# Regulatory Landscape Research Domain Template

Structure for deep research of regulatory environments and compliance requirements.

---

## Subtopics for Decomposition

1. **Current Regulatory Framework**
   - Primary legislation and directives
   - Implementing regulations and technical standards
   - Supervisory authorities and their guidance
   - Enforcement history and precedents

2. **Upcoming Changes**
   - Proposed legislation in pipeline
   - Expected implementation timelines
   - Transition periods and grandfathering clauses
   - Industry consultation status

3. **Compliance Requirements**
   - Mandatory requirements (must-have)
   - Reporting obligations and deadlines
   - Documentation and record-keeping
   - Audit and certification requirements

4. **Industry Impact**
   - Affected entities and scope
   - Cost of compliance estimates
   - Competitive implications (barriers vs. levelers)
   - Cross-border considerations

5. **Implementation Approaches**
   - Best practices from early adopters
   - Technology solutions for compliance
   - Common pitfalls and failures
   - Phased implementation strategies

## Credibility Sources for Regulatory Research

### High (+3)
- Official gazette / legislation databases (EUR-Lex, legislation.gov)
- Regulatory authority publications (EBA, ESMA, FCA, SEC)
- Official consultation papers and impact assessments

### High (+2)
- Big 4 regulatory alerts (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG)
- Law firm client alerts (major firms)
- Industry association position papers

### Medium (+1)
- Compliance technology vendors (may have bias but current data)
- Academic papers on regulatory impact
- Conference presentations by regulators

### Lower (0)
- General news articles about regulation
- Social media commentary
