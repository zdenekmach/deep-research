# Wildlife Photography Research Domain Template

Structure for deep research of wildlife photography destinations.

---

## Subtopics for Decomposition

For wildlife photography research, always decompose into these areas:

1. **Ecosystem & Terrain**
   - Biotopes and habitats
   - Light conditions (forest shade, open plains)
   - Seasonal landscape changes
   - Geographic specifics

2. **Wildlife — Target Species**
   - Priority species for photography
   - Behavior and activity (daily cycle)
   - Occurrence locations
   - Seasonal variations (migration, breeding, feeding)

3. **Photography Technique**
   - Recommended lenses
   - Camera settings (aperture, shutter, ISO)
   - Composition strategies
   - Support (tripod, bean bag, gimbal)

4. **Seasonal Strategy**
   - Best periods for different species
   - Weather and light conditions
   - Special events (migration, fruiting seasons)
   - Off-peak advantages

5. **Practical Logistics**
   - Safari operators and guides
   - Prices and entry fees
   - Park rules and permits
   - Accommodation

## Credibility Sources for Wildlife

### High (+2 to +3)
- National Geographic, BBC Wildlife
- IUCN Red List, UNESCO World Heritage
- National park official websites
- Scientific journals (Mammalian Biology, Journal of Wildlife Management)

### Medium (+1)
- Wildlife Worldwide, Natural Habitat Adventures
- Professional wildlife photographer blogs
- Established safari operator websites

### Lower (0 to -1)
- TripAdvisor reviews, Reddit
- General travel blogs, social media

## Specific Transformations

### Behavior → Photography Opportunity

```
Fact: "Leopards are crepuscular, most active at dawn and dusk"

Transform:
"Leopards most active 6:00-9:00 and 15:30-18:00.
Settings: f/5.6-f/8 (sufficient depth for movement),
1/500s minimum (freeze motion), ISO auto 800-6400.
AF-C/AI Servo with tracking. Position: side of jeep facing action."
```

### Habitat → Technique

```
Fact: "Natural lakes with sand rims"

Transform:
"Sand-rimmed lakes = natural reflectors. White sand bounces light
onto undersides of animals. Compose low for mirror reflections.
Exposure: +0.7 to +1.3 EV compensation. Polarizer for glare reduction."
```
