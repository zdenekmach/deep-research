# Market Entry Research Domain Template

Structure for deep research of new market entry opportunities.

---

## Subtopics for Decomposition

Always decompose market entry research into these streams:

1. **Market Size & Dynamics**
   - TAM / SAM / SOM estimation
   - Growth rate and trajectory
   - Market maturity (emerging / growing / mature / declining)
   - Seasonality and cyclicality

2. **Customer Landscape**
   - Target segments and personas
   - Buying behavior and decision process
   - Pain points and unmet needs
   - Willingness to pay

3. **Competitive Environment**
   - Direct competitors (who, market share, positioning)
   - Indirect competitors and substitutes
   - Barriers to entry (capital, regulation, network effects)
   - Competitive advantages available

4. **Regulatory & Compliance**
   - Industry-specific regulations
   - Licensing and certification requirements
   - Data protection and privacy (GDPR, local laws)
   - Tax implications

5. **Go-to-Market**
   - Distribution channels
   - Partnership opportunities
   - Pricing benchmarks
   - Marketing channels that work in this market

## Credibility Sources for Market Research

### High (+2 to +3)
- Gartner, Forrester, McKinsey Global Institute
- Statista (with methodology notes)
- Industry associations and trade bodies
- Government statistics (census, trade data)
- SEC filings, annual reports

### Medium (+1)
- CB Insights, Crunchbase, PitchBook
- Industry-specific publications
- Analyst blogs (named authors with track record)

### Lower (0 to -1)
- Generic market research summaries without methodology
- Press releases (treat as company claims, not facts)
- Quora, Reddit (directional signal only)
